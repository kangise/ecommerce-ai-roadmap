## Research Methodology (not platform rules)

The following are domain-specific research constraints used in product selection:
- **Demand floor**: Minimum monthly search volume to consider a product viable (typically 300+ for Amazon US)
- **Competition ceiling**: Maximum number of competing listings with 100+ reviews before market is considered saturated
- **Margin floor**: Minimum target profit margin (typically 30%+) after all fees to qualify a product
- **Review velocity**: Products in the niche should receive at least X reviews/month to indicate organic demand
- **Seasonality check**: Avoid products where >40% of annual sales occur in a single quarter
- **IP risk exclusion**: Products with active patents or trademarks in the category require FTO analysis before proceeding
- **Supplier diversification**: Never rely on a single supplier; target 2-3 qualified suppliers per product
